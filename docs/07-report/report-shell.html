About this report

Mooney's is an online bookstore built for the CSCE310 course project. It has three backend services and two Windows desktop apps. The user-service (Java, MySQL) keeps accounts and logs people in. The book-service (Python Flask, MongoDB) keeps the books, the stock and the reviews. The order-service (Python Flask, MySQL) takes orders and rentals, and emails the bill. Customers use the "Mooney's" app; managers use the "Mooney's Manager" app.

This report covers what the course asks for: the database design, the REST API, the main screens, and the architecture and classes. Section 5 links the videos of the user acceptance tests. Every fact in it was taken from the code and the database schema as they are in the version on the cover.

The full process is in the repository: the idea and requirements in docs/01-idea, the design in docs/02-design, the problems met and the improvements made in docs/03-problems, the plan in docs/04-planning, the setup in docs/05-setup, and the requirement tests in docs/06-testing. INSTALL.md explains how to install and run everything.

7.3 Data and other sources

  • Book cover photos: Open Library Covers API, Internet Archive, https://openlibrary.org/dev/docs/api/covers. Linked by address, not copied.
  • Book titles and authors: real, published books. Prices, copies, customers, orders, and reviews are invented test data.
  • The store's name and sign follow the fictional bookshop "Mooney's" in the series You; the sign is drawn by the app's own code.
  • Use of an AI assistant: much of the code, documents, and diagrams was written with Claude (Anthropic), directed by the student, who made every decision. Details in REFERENCES.md.
